Academic homepage

Hi, I’m Junting Wu, an undergraduate majoring in honors mathematics at NYU Shanghai. My research interests include Bayesian inverse problems, sampling algorithms, and uncertainty quantification. I’m particularly interested in developing accurate and scalable algorithms for high-dimensional inference.

I’ve been fortunate to work with Professor Yifan Chen (UCLA) and Professor Georg Stadler (NYU’s Courant Institute). With Prof. Chen, I helped develop a Gaussian mixture variational inference algorithm that switches between Wasserstein and Fisher–Rao updates. During my junior year, I studied away in New York and completed an independent study with Prof. Stadler on inverse problems and Gaussian process methods. I’m now working with him on my senior thesis on passive inverse problems.

I’m grateful to Professor Jun Zhang for introducing me to applied mathematics research through a project on the open siphon phenomenon in soap films at NYU Shanghai’s Applied Math Lab (AML).
